01Description of the Service

Arciom is a paid-media governance and expertise-capture platform built for advertising agencies and the client accounts they manage. The Service operates on a multi-tenant "agency model": an agency deployment hosts one or more accounts (the agency's clients), and each account has one or more users with defined roles.

The Service generally includes:

  • Governance monitoring — connecting to a Customer's own Google Ads, Google Analytics, and/or Google Tag Manager accounts (via Google OAuth) to detect material changes, prompt the responsible buyer to record reasoning and confidence, track outcomes over time, and generate AI-assisted summaries.
  • Capture and digital twin — an optional desktop capture agent and/or voice debrief feature that records screen activity, audio narration, click events, window/application titles, and typed context notes during a working session, and uses AI models to reconstruct decision points and build a persistent record of a user's expertise ("digital twin").
  • Administrative and reporting tools made available to agency admins, account owners, and system administrators.

Arciom may add, change, deprecate, or remove features at any time, particularly during the Beta Period described in Section 3.
