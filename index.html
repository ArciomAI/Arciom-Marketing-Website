The twin's braingrowing

The current situation

AI can copy what your experts know. It can't copy how they decide.

Every model available today has already absorbed the general knowledge of your industry. That's not your edge anymore — it's the baseline everyone starts from.

What every AI model knows
Commodity

Best practices, benchmarks, the playbook. Every competitor starts from the same place you do.

VS
Your experts' judgment
Scarce

Knowing whether a spike is auction pressure or ad fatigue. The exceptions to the rules. The calls no model would make — that turn out right.

How judgment is lost

It leaves with people

Retirement, turnover, promotion. Years of calibrated decision-making walks out on a two-week notice, and the team relearns what was already known.

The reasoning isn't captured

SOPs, wikis, and screen recordings document what happened. They never capture why — the context, the tradeoffs, the intuition behind the call.

Experts can't explain instinct

Psychologists call it the expert blind spot: people can't articulate most of their own decision logic. It only surfaces while they're actually doing the work.

The shift

Every time a new AI model ships, what your team knows gets more common, and how they decide gets more valuable.

Your edge is the judgment you haven't captured yet. Every month you wait is judgment made, used once, and lost.

How it works

What a digital twin actually is.

Not a chatbot with your logo on it. It runs on one loop: capture, prove, grow. A decision is only kept if it beats the frontier model.

Step 01 · Capture

Capture

Arciom records your marketer working: screen and voice, thinking out loud. A short conversation after each session captures the reasoning behind the calls.

Step 02 · Prove

Prove

Every decision is tested against a frontier model you choose — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini. If the model would have made the same call, it's discarded. That knowledge is free everywhere.

Step 03 · Grow

Grow

Only proven decisions accumulate. With enough of them — and the permissions you grant — that judgment grows into a digital twin tied to your expert, capable of real work.

✓ kept your expert beat the model   ✕ discarded the model would have made the same call
And it keeps sharpening. Arciom tracks the result of every change, so the digital twin sees cause and effect. Winning judgments gain weight; failing ones get flagged and re-examined. It learns from the market, not just the expert.

Our process · the trust ladder

Autonomy is earned. Rung by rung.

You don't hand over the account. The digital twin operates through permissions you grant, and every step up is earned by evidence.

RUNG 01

Incubate

Watches real decisions. Connects cause to effect. Advises no one.

RUNG 02

Recommend

Suggests changes. Your expert accepts, rejects, or improves them.

RUNG 03

Assist

Drafts the change itself. Nothing ships without human sign-off.

RUNG 04

Run

Handles routine work. Your expert keeps the calls only they can make.

⏻  A human gate at every rung — stop it anywhere, anytime

Your marketer isn't replaced by their digital twin. They spend less time on repetitive execution and more on the judgment calls only they can make.
